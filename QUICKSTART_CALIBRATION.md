# Sensor Calibration - Quick Start

## What is it?

The sensor calibration feature automatically adjusts for different mounting orientations of the ICM-20948 sensor on your golf club head. No matter how you mount the sensor, calibration ensures the data always represents the club's actual movement.

## Quick Steps

### Option 1: Full Calibration (2 minutes, most accurate)

1. **Connect** your sensor via USB or Bluetooth
2. **Click** "Calibrate Sensor" button
3. **Click** "Start Calibration" 
4. Follow on-screen prompts for 4 positions:
   - **Address** - Normal club address position
   - **Toe Up** - Club horizontal, toe pointing up
   - **Toe Down** - Club horizontal, toe pointing down  
   - **Face Up** - Club on its back, face pointing up
5. Hold steady for 2 seconds at each position
6. Done! Calibration is saved automatically

### Option 2: Simple Calibration (30 seconds, basic accuracy)

1. **Connect** your sensor
2. **Click** "Calibrate Sensor" button
3. **Click** "Simple Calibration"
4. Hold club at **address position**
5. Click "Collect" and hold steady for 2 seconds
6. Done!

## When to Calibrate

✅ **Calibrate when:**
- First time setting up the sensor
- After remounting the sensor
- Data seems incorrect or rotated

❌ **No need to calibrate:**
- Between swing sessions (if mount unchanged)
- After disconnecting/reconnecting
- For each new user

## Tips

- Hold club **very steady** during collection
- Avoid **metal surfaces** under the club
- Keep away from **magnetic interference**
- If calibration fails, try again or use Simple Calibration

## Checking Calibration Status

Look for **"Status: Calibrated"** in the Sensor Calibration section of the main window.
